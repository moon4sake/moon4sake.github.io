I am a Ph.D. student in the SIML lab at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Juho Lee.

I am interested in building efficient language-model agents. Agents today rely on ever-larger models. I aim to build small agents that adaptively select which capabilities of large models to retain. My research spans pruning, knowledge distillation, and agent harness. Beyond efficiency, I also study how to make models reliable.

I am on the job market for positions starting in Spring 2027. I have one year of alternative military service (Technical Research Personnel) remaining.
