I am a Ph.D. student in the SIML lab at KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Juho Lee.

My research has two directions.

  • Building efficient language-model agents. Agents today rely on ever-larger models. I aim to build small agents that adaptively select which capabilities of large models to retain. My research spans pruning, knowledge distillation, and agent harness. (arXiv’26, NeurIPS’26, ICML’24, ICLR’24, ICMLW’23, NeurIPSW’22)
  • Building reliable models. Capability alone does not make a model trustworthy. I also aim to build models that are well calibrated and safe. (ICML’26, ICMLW’25, ECCV’24, arXiv’24, ICMLW’23, arXiv’22)

I am on the job market for positions starting in Spring 2027. I have one year of alternative military service (Technical Research Personnel) remaining.
